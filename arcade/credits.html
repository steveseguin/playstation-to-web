Compiled games

Paddle Rally and Brick Shift compile directly from C to WebAssembly and run without an emulator. Harvest Moon: Back to Nature is listed to explain the compiler's first supported game. Its binaries, images, music, and other assets are not included. Players must build it locally and supply their own matching disc.
